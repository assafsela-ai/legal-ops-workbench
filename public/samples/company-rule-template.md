# Company rule template

Paste one rule at a time into **Documents & playbook → Add rule**.

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Rule name | Vendor training exclusion |
| Rule text | Customer recordings must be excluded from vendor model training. |

Use your own internally approved wording. Update rules through **Edit rule** so that decisions retain links to the version they used.

## Fictional sample rules

- **AI training.** Customer recordings must be excluded from vendor model training.
- **Liability.** Customer indemnity stays under the liability cap, except for intentional misconduct.
- **Retention.** Recording retention above 30 days requires a new Privacy approval before launch.
